Moving to a new phone

Your progress is stored on your device and travels with an iCloud or computer backup when you restore it onto a new phone.

Reminders

Turn study reminders on or off, or change their time, in More → Settings. If they don’t appear, check that notifications are allowed for the app in iOS Settings.

About the test

The Life in the UK test has 24 questions, 45 minutes, and a pass mark of 18 (75%). Book only through the official GOV.UK service, and check GOV.UK for current fees and rules.
