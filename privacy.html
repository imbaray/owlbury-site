Privacy policy

Owlbury for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 3 November 2026

In short: we don’t collect your name, email address, study progress or any account details. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only things that leave your device are the update check described below, any email you choose to send us, and any web page you choose to open from a link.

Who we are

Owlbury is made by Rui Mamede, an independent developer in the United Kingdom, trading as Owlbury (“we”). You can contact us at support@owlbury.co.uk.

Your study progress

Your answers, mock test results, flashcard schedule and settings (such as your test date) are stored only on your device, inside the app. They are included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups if you have backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple account, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and “Reset all progress” in Settings erases it at any time.

App updates

So that corrections to questions and notes reach you quickly, the app checks for an update when it starts and when you return to it (at most every 30 minutes). It uses Expo’s update service (EAS Update, run by 650 Industries, Inc. in the United States), which processes this data for us. Each check includes:

  • a random identifier created for this installation (a new one is made if you delete and reinstall the app);
  • your IP address, as with any internet connection;
  • the app’s version and channel, and which update it is running;
  • your device’s operating system;
  • if an update failed to start, the IDs of the failed updates and a short technical error message (crash data).

This is used only to send the right update and to give us counts of how many installations received an update and whether it started correctly. We can’t see your identifier or IP address, and none of it is linked to your identity or used for advertising or tracking. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in giving you correct study material.

Expo processes this data for us under its terms of service, which make it our data processor, and gives it the same or equal protection as this policy. Expo may also use it in aggregated or de-identified form to improve its own services, as its privacy policy describes (expo.dev/privacy). Expo keeps the data only for as long as reasonably necessary, and says it complies with the UK Extension to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. To stop the update check, delete the app; reinstalling it creates a new identifier.

Notifications

If you turn on study reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS. No push notification service or server is involved, and you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Pictures and links to other websites

The pictures in the app come from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain or Creative Commons licences; each one is credited under the picture and in Settings → Picture credits. They are built into the app, so looking at them needs no internet connection and sends nothing.

Some pages have links, such as “Read more on Wikipedia” or links to GOV.UK. A page opens (in Safari, inside the app) only when you tap its link. That website then receives what any website receives, such as your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy policy: for example the Wikimedia Foundation’s or GOV.UK’s. The app itself doesn’t see what you do there. Owlbury is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.

People named in the app

The notes, timeline and “Key people” pages name people from the official handbook, most of them historical and some living public figures, with short factual summaries of their public lives. We include this information because it is what the Life in the UK test covers (our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing the study material). We never show photographs of identifiable living people. If you are named and something is wrong, or you would like it changed, email us and we will correct it.

Emails you send us

If you contact us, for example with “Report a problem” (which opens your own email app with the question number filled in), we receive what you choose to send: your email address and your message. We use it only to reply and to correct the app’s content, we never share it, and we delete it once it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The app is a study aid for adults preparing for the Life in the UK test. It doesn’t knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. In practice that is only emails you have sent us: the update service’s records use a random identifier that we can’t link to you. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever started collecting any further data, we would say so here and in the App Store’s privacy details before that version is released.

Owlbury is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Home Office or HM Government.
